## D. Kaise apply karein (checklist)

- [ ] Subah 10 min: nayi listings check (upar wale links)
- [ ] 10 tailored proposals (template `profiles/upwork-fiverr.md` se)
- [ ] Har proposal me aapka **portfolio link** + client ka naam
- [ ] 1 ghante me reply — speed zyada matter karta hai
- [ ] Sham ko: jinhe reply aaya, unke liye follow-up
- [ ] Shuruat me $20-50 ke chhote gigs lo — review sabse important hai

## E. Reality check

- Ek gig par 60–300 bids hote hain — **normal** hai. 100 proposals me 2-3 reply aate hain.
- Jo "no experience" bolte hain, wo bhi **proof** (portfolio/git) maangte hain.
- Pehla gig milne me 1-3 hafte lag sakte hain. **Consistency sabse bada factor hai.**
- Kabhi bhi "pay to work" ya advance fee mat do.
